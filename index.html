About the project

A closer, more transparent look at the retina.

Diabetic retinopathy is a leading cause of preventable blindness. This project explores how AI can support earlier detection, while keeping every decision visible and explainable.

Retinal fundus photo
Diabetic Retinopathy

What it is

Diabetic retinopathy is damage to the blood vessels of the retina caused by prolonged high blood sugar. It often develops silently, with mild leaks and swelling that are easy to miss until vision is already affected — making regular screening essential.

AI screening scan
AI-based early detection

How our system helps

Our model is trained on retinal imagery to recognise early markers of diabetic retinopathy — such as microaneurysms and small haemorrhages — and to flag findings for a clinician's review, aiming to support faster, more consistent screening.

Grad-CAM overlay
Grad-CAM heatmaps

Seeing what the model sees

Grad-CAM highlights the exact regions of the retina that most influenced the AI's prediction, overlaid as a colour heatmap. Warmer areas indicate stronger influence, letting clinicians visually verify what the model is responding to.

Clinician review
Explainable AI

Why explainability matters

In healthcare, a prediction alone isn't enough. Explainable AI turns the model's internal reasoning into visual, human-readable evidence — building trust between the system, the clinician, and the patient.
